D.I.V.E.

Platform:
PC
Team:
8 members
My role:
Art lead/Game design

D.I.V.E. is a PC fast-paced 2D action platformer launched free on itch.io, players can control a cyber world bounty hunter to solve the puzzles and combat in quick action. The game is designed for fast-moving, or “blink combat”. The main game mechanic is the Dash Attack. With this super mobility, the player can solve the platformer puzzle and change position really quickly. In combat, the player can use the dash attack to reposition and backstab the enemies before they react.
